# Icon source attribution

The default tray icon for `hermes-mnemosyne-tray` is derived from:

> **Mosaïque murale : Mnémosyne** — Roman wall mosaic, 2nd c. AD
> National Archaeological Museum of Tarragona (Inv. MNAT 45252-2)
> Photographed by Caroline Léna Becker
> https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mosa%C3%AFque_murale_Mn%C3%A9mosyne.jpg
> **CC0 (public domain dedication)**

Center-cropped to square and circular-masked. No other modifications.
